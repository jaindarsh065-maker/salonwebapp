The appointment

What an hour here actually looks like.

01

You pick the time, we hold it

Choosing a slot reserves the chair for exactly as long as your service needs, plus the turnaround after it. That time stops being offered to anyone else the moment you confirm.

02

Seven days, ten till ten

Open every day of the week, so an appointment can fit around work rather than the other way round.

Women and men, same room
